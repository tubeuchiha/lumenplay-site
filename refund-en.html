Refund Policy

LumenPlay · Effective: 2026-11-16 · Version 1.0

In one sentence: there is a 7-day full-feature free trial — try before you buy. An activation code that has never been activated is fully refundable within 14 days; an activated digital licence is non-refundable. All refund requests go through support@lumenplay.app.

1. Try before you buy

LumenPlay offers a 7-day full-feature free trial: it starts the first time you use any paid capability (bitstream passthrough, ISO / BDMV disc playback, ASS / SSA styled subtitles), unlocks everything for 7 days, and requires no sign-up or account. Paid capabilities depend on your device and AV hardware, so please confirm support during the trial before purchasing.

2. Refund conditions

Situation Outcome
Code purchased less than 14 days ago and never activated on any deviceFull refund
A fault on our side makes the code unusable and it cannot be fixed through supportFull refund (no time limit)

Refund requested after the code has been activated (bound to a device)Not available (see section 3)
A paid capability is unavailable because the device does not support itNot a refund case (see section 4)

3. Why activated licences are non-refundable

LumenPlay is digital content: once an activation code is used, delivery is complete and the licence is bound to a device, so it cannot be recalled. Activating a code means you consent to immediate performance and waive the 14-day right of withdrawal — consistent with Article 16 of the EU Consumer Rights Directive on digital content. We deliver nothing before you activate; after activation, refunds are limited to the exceptions in section 2 (a fault on our side).

4. Unsupported hardware is not a defect

Paid capabilities depend on hardware: bitstream passthrough requires the device and the AV receiver to support the same audio codec; Dolby Vision requires a compatible display and decode path. When hardware does not support a capability, the App degrades gracefully (audio decoded to PCM, Dolby Vision played as its HDR10 base layer) and videos still play in full. This is not a product defect and is not grounds for a refund. The website, the App and the purchase pages all state "depending on device support" — please verify during the trial.

5. How refunds work per channel

  • Google Play: purchases are processed entirely by Google. Use "Order history → Request a refund" in the Play Store under Google's refund policy; we cannot act on your behalf.
  • Website (paid via Paddle): Paddle acts as merchant of record and processes payments and refunds. Email support@lumenplay.app and we will refund through Paddle to the original payment method. Eligible refunds are processed within 5 business days of confirmation; banks and wallets typically post them within 3–10 days.
  • Website (Alipay / WeChat transfer + email): eligible refunds are returned to the original payment account, also within 5 business days of confirmation.

6. How to request a refund

Email support@lumenplay.app with the subject "Refund request" and include:

  • purchase date and payment channel;
  • order number or proof of payment (Paddle receipt / transfer screenshot);
  • the activation code, if one was issued;
  • a one-line reason.

We usually reply within 24 hours. Eligible refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 business days of confirmation, and the activation code is revoked at the same time (or invalidated immediately if never activated).

7. Miscellaneous

  • This policy expands section 5 of the Terms of Service; if they conflict, the Terms of Service prevail.
  • We may update this policy; changes are published on this page with an effective date. Refund requests are handled under the policy version in force at the time of purchase.
  • If the consumer law of your country grants stronger refund rights, those mandatory provisions are unaffected by this policy.
