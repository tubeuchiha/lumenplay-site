Privacy Policy

LumenPlay · Effective: 2026-11-19 · Version 1.4

In short: no accounts, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of what you watch. The App connects to the internet for only two purposes: the media sources you add yourself, and entitlement checks (sections 1 and 2). Entitlement checks retain a limited set of licensing information on our server (section 1); they contain nothing about what, when or how long you watch.

1. What information we handle

The App never sends your personal information to any server. The following is stored only on your device and used solely for the App's own features:

  • Playback history / recently played: kept in the App's private directory, used for the "recently played" list;
  • Media server credentials (Jellyfin / WebDAV / SMB addresses, usernames, passwords): passwords are encrypted with AES-GCM via the Android KeyStore and stored locally. They never leave the device and are excluded from system cloud backups;
  • Playback preferences (aspect-ratio memory, subtitle preferences, etc.): stored locally;
  • Device code: to keep a paid licence tied to the right device, the App derives a device code that contains no personal identity (an irreversible hash that cannot be traced back to you or your device). Raw device identifiers never leave the device; our servers store only this code, used to attribute licences and enforce the device count, and never shared with third parties.
  • Licence status (direct channel only): when you use a paid feature (disc image / Dolby passthrough / styled subtitles), the App confirms with our licence server whether this device is entitled, and renews automatically while the licence is valid. To do this the server records the device code, purchase record and validity period — used to attribute the licence, to revoke access after a refund or cancellation, and to prevent one purchase from being used on more devices than allowed. This is the minimum licensing information, comparable to a purchase ledger, and contains nothing about what, when or how long you watch. Users who have not purchased are also recorded — at the start of the trial the server stores the device code and trial start time, cleared after a retention period once the trial ends. Licence requests are sent over HTTPS.
  • Licence re-check: while playing paid content the App performs a read-only check to confirm the licence has not been revoked. This check carries only the device code and is not additionally logged by the server.

2. Where network traffic goes

Apart from the licence checks described above (entitlement confirmation and the revocation re-check, carrying only licensing information), network traffic from the App only occurs between your device and the media sources you add yourself (your NAS, your Windows share, your Jellyfin server, or a video URL you enter). We run no relay servers, so no third party can see what you are watching. The licence check carries no viewing activity or content information.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: play network video; detect connectivity (Wi-Fi, disconnection) for adaptive bitrate and offline notices;
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE: used only to discover media servers on the local network (NAS, SMB shares, Jellyfin/Emby, DLNA). Android drops Wi-Fi multicast packets by default to save power, so the App must briefly hold a multicast lock to receive discovery broadcasts — the lock is held only for the seconds a scan runs and released immediately afterwards. It enables no continuous listening and involves no location data (the App requests no location permission);
  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: list local videos on your device;
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: same purpose, requested only on Android 12 and earlier (newer versions use the granular media permission above);
  • WAKE_LOCK: keep screen/network latency low during playback;
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: switch audio routing for Dolby passthrough;
  • CAMERA: opened only when you choose "Scan to activate another device", to read a one-time activation QR code shown on another device. No photos, no video, nothing uploaded; the camera is never accessed otherwise, and devices without a camera can install and use the app normally.

4. Open-source components

The App uses FFmpeg (LGPL-2.1+, dynamically linked), OpenSSL, libxml2, OkHttp, Coil, SMBJ, AndroidX, Material Components, SLF4J, and a few open-source cryptography components (public domain or permissive licences) used for licence verification. The licence list is on the Open-source licences page. These components run locally and perform no independent data collection.

5. Purchases through Google Play

When you buy the Pro features through the Google Play channel, payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card number or billing address. Google only tells us whether a given user has purchased, without identity details. Google Play purchases are licensed entirely by Google Play's result — no licence check is performed and no device code is uploaded on this channel.

6. Changes to this policy

If any data collection is ever introduced (there is no such plan today), it will be announced prominently on this page in advance and reflected in the App's own notices.

7. Contact

For any privacy or data-protection question, contact support@lumenplay.app (usually answered within 24 hours).
