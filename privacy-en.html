Privacy Policy

LumenPlay · Effective: 2026-11-13 · Version 1.2

In one sentence: we do not collect, upload or analyse any user data. No accounts, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs.

1. What information we handle

The App never sends your personal information to any server. The following is stored only on your device and used solely for the App's own features:

  • Playback history / recently played: kept in the App's private directory, used for the "recently played" list;
  • Media server credentials (Jellyfin / WebDAV / SMB addresses, usernames, passwords): passwords are encrypted with AES-GCM via the Android KeyStore and stored locally. They never leave the device and are excluded from system cloud backups;
  • Playback preferences (aspect-ratio memory, subtitle preferences, etc.): stored locally;
  • Device identifier (ANDROID_ID): used only on-device to derive the "machine code" for activation-code licensing on our website channel. It is never uploaded and never communicated to any server, and cannot be used by us or any third party to identify you.

2. Where network traffic goes

Network traffic from the App only occurs between your device and the media sources you add yourself (your NAS, your Windows share, your Jellyfin server, or a video URL you enter). We run no relay servers, so no third party can see what you are watching.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: play network video; detect connectivity (Wi-Fi, disconnection) for adaptive bitrate and offline notices;
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE: used only to discover media servers on the local network (NAS, SMB shares, Jellyfin/Emby, DLNA). Android drops Wi-Fi multicast packets by default to save power, so the App must briefly hold a multicast lock to receive discovery broadcasts — the lock is held only for the seconds a scan runs and released immediately afterwards. It enables no continuous listening and involves no location data (the App requests no location permission);
  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: list local videos on your device;
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: same purpose, requested only on Android 12 and earlier (newer versions use the granular media permission above);
  • WAKE_LOCK: keep screen/network latency low during playback;
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: switch audio routing for Dolby passthrough.

4. Open-source components

The App uses FFmpeg (LGPL-2.1+, dynamically linked), OpenSSL, libxml2, OkHttp, Coil, SMBJ, AndroidX, Material Components and SLF4J. The licence list is on the Open-source licences page. These components run locally and perform no independent data collection.

5. Purchases through Google Play

When you buy the Pro features through the Google Play channel, payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card number or billing address. Google only tells us whether a given user has purchased, without identity details.

6. Changes to this policy

If any data collection is ever introduced (there is no such plan today), it will be announced prominently on this page in advance and reflected in the App's own notices.

7. Contact

For any privacy or data-protection question, contact support@lumenplay.app (usually answered within 24 hours).
