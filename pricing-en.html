3. 7-day full-feature trial

Starting the first time you use any paid capability, everything is unlocked for 7 days — no sign-up, no account, no payment details required. When the trial ends, the App returns to the free feature set and videos still play in full. Please confirm device support during the trial before purchasing.

4. Hardware prerequisites for paid capabilities

  • Bitstream passthrough: requires the device and AV receiver to support the same codec (TrueHD / DTS-HD, etc.). Otherwise audio is decoded to PCM and playback is unaffected.
  • Dolby Vision: requires a compatible display and decode path. Sources with an HDR10 compatibility layer (Profile 8) fall back to the base layer; Profile 5 has no fallback layer and the App clearly warns when the device does not support it.
  • All capabilities depend on device hardware; missing support is not a defect. See section 4 of the Refund Policy.

5. Taxes and payment

  • Google Play channel: handled by Google; applicable taxes are collected by Google under local rules.
  • Website (via Paddle): Paddle acts as merchant of record and calculates, collects and remits applicable taxes based on the buyer's location. Available currencies and payment methods (Alipay / WeChat Pay / cards, etc.) are shown at checkout.
  • Website (Alipay / WeChat transfer): personal transfer, all fees included in the listed price.

6. Price changes

We may adjust prices or run promotions; completed purchases are never affected by later price changes (the licence is perpetual and includes free updates). Promotional prices are not applied retroactively and are not grounds for a refund.
