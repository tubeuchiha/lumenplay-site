Activation now happens inside the app

No web page, no order ID, nothing to copy or type.

  1. On a phone or tablet that is already unlocked, open LumenPlay;
  2. Go to Playback settings → Paid features and tap Scan to activate another device;
  3. Scan the QR code shown on the device you want to activate. It unlocks within seconds.

The device you want to activate shows that QR code (and a short number) in its unlock window.
